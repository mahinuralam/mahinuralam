## About me

I'm a Ph.D. student in Information Systems at the **University of Maryland, Baltimore County (UMBC)**, advised by [Dr. Sanjay Purushotham](https://sanjayp.is.umbc.edu/). I currently work on **federated reasoning LLMs**: building a framework to resolve *reasoning collapse* in federated LLMs trained on heterogeneous data.

Before UMBC, I was a Graduate Research Assistant at [PIC Lab](https://sites.google.com/view/piclabkit/home), Kumoh National Institute of Technology (South Korea), advised by Dr. Taesoo Jun, where I worked on industrial AI, multimodal learning, and digital-twin-based warehouse automation. I also spent a semester at Georgia Southern University on tiny-object detection for UAV imagery, and worked as a Software Engineer at Divine IT Ltd. My research has been published in **JISA, IEEE Access, ICT Express, ICRA (workshop), ICC, ICMIC, and ICTC**.
